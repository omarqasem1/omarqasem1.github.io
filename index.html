About

I am an Assistant Professor of Electrical Engineering in the Electrical and Computer Engineering Department, School of Engineering and Computing, at American International University in Kuwait. I received my Ph.D. from the Florida Institute of Technology in 2023.

My research develops adaptive dynamic programming (ADP) and reinforcement learning methods that learn optimal controllers directly from data. A central thread is hybrid iteration (HI), a learning framework that, unlike policy iteration, needs no initial stabilizing controller, while converging faster than value iteration. I apply these methods to multi-agent systems, microgrids with inverter-based resources, quantized and time-varying systems, and robotic platforms.

Research areas

01

Adaptive dynamic programming

Hybrid iteration methods that bridge value and policy iteration, with admissibility guarantees for linear and nonlinear systems.

02

Learning-based optimal control

Data-driven controllers for multi-agent, output-feedback, quantized, time-varying and delayed systems.

03

Power systems

Cooperative output regulation in islanded microgrids with IBRs, and ADP-based droop control.
